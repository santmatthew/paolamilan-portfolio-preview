About the exhibition

An experience where color becomes visible emotion

In The Language of Gesture (2026), color and paint become a direct language of emotion. Each work captures moments of energy and presence, where inner impulses are translated into painterly gesture.

The viewer enters an immersive space within the movement of color and the density of paint, where emotion becomes visible and presence becomes physical.

The works build living surfaces: color expands, paint breathes, and the pictorial field activates the space, creating an experience in constant transformation.

Inspired by the freedom of gesture and the energy of the brushstroke, the series proposes a quiet, attentive contemplation, where each color leaves a lasting trace.

The exhibition brings together 18 new works that span large canvases and intimate formats, all created between 2025 and 2026.

How to get there

48 Art Studio is located at Calle General Pardiñas 48, in the heart of Madrid’s Salamanca district — one of the city’s most vibrant and elegant areas.

The gallery is very easy to reach and is surrounded by several metro stations within walking distance, including Goya, Lista, Núñez de Balboa, and Diego de León, connecting you quickly to anywhere in Madrid.

Whether you’re coming from the city centre or elsewhere, it’s a smooth and direct journey into one of Madrid’s most dynamic cultural neighbourhoods
